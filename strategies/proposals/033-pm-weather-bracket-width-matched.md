## Why this might fail

The arithmetic is real but the margin is thin and it is destroyed by point forecast bias, which is a different error from RMSE. Centred, a 5.4F bracket at sigma 2.74 caps at 2*Phi(5.4 / 5.48) - 1 = 2*Phi(0.985) - 1 = 0.676, against 2*Phi(1.8 / 5.48) - 1 = 0.257 for a single 1.8F rung, so the width matching argument works. But if mu is off by 1.0F, p_bracket falls to Phi(3.7/2.74) - Phi(-1.7/2.74) = 0.9115 - 0.2676 = 0.644, and a 4 cent gate on a 0.64 cost becomes roughly a 0.4 cent real edge, or 61 bps, well under the 200 bps floor. My 380 bps estimate assumes a 0.7F mean absolute centring error: p_bracket 0.660 against a centred 0.676, so 1.56 cents of the 4 cent gate is eaten and 2.44 cents survive on a 0.64 basket, which is 381 bps. If the real centring error is 1.0F this is a losing strategy and the record cannot currently tell me which it is, because RMSE conflates bias and variance and nobody has decomposed it. Second failure mode: 2.74F is an RMSE per station-day, and treating it as a Gaussian sigma assumes symmetric, unbiased, normal errors. Temperature forecast errors are skewed around frontal passages, and the tail that busts the bracket is exactly the correlated one. Third: 3 legs into a thin book across one poll cycle is the corridor one-legged-fill problem multiplied, and the unwind rule costs a full spread on every partial. Fourth: 1 bracket per day per station means reaching 40 resolved brackets takes weeks, so the likely honest outcome is NOT_TESTED for a long time.

## What past failure this addresses

This is not a re-proposal of anything in the hypothesis graph, and I want to be precise about why. PM_weather_arb has never fired, so it is NOT_TESTED, not failed (convention 11), and the deterministic half of Forge already writes shadow_unblock_weather_arb as a repair, which this deliberately does not duplicate: that repair unblocks the existing rung strategy, this changes the instrument. The relevant record entry is the live skip reason rung_narrower_than_model_resolution at 2,220 skips, whose own text names the fix as a narrower predictor rather than a narrower threshold. I am proposing the third option that text does not consider, a wider instrument, and the cap arithmetic above is the justification. Vault notes leaned on: corridor_pair_live.md, from which I take the rule that a one-legged fill has no floor and the discipline of measuring a multi-leg structure against binned fair value rather than a round number, and 2026-08-18-corridor-pair-works.md, whose superseded risk-free framing is exactly the mistake I avoid here by pricing the bracket against p_bracket rather than against 1.00. I also take from 2026-08-18-cycle-001-day-1-lessons.md the rule not to treat a never-fired strategy as a failed idea, which is why weather gets a new instrument rather than a lowered threshold.

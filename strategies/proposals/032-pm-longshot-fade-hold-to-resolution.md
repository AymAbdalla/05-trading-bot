## Why this might fail

This is picking up pennies in front of a possible steamroller and the arithmetic is brutally sensitive to the sigma estimate. One loss at 0.96 wipes out 32 wins of 3 cents. Concretely: BTC 15m sigma of 22 bps, 3 minutes left, sigma_rem = 22 * sqrt(0.2) = 9.8 bps. At a true 30 bps distance z = 3.06 and p_tail = 0.11%, but after subtracting the 5 bps proxy noise floor z = 2.55 and p_tail = 0.54%, a fivefold change in the loss rate from a 5 bps instrument error. If realized crypto returns are fat tailed rather than Gaussian, which they are, the true p_tail at z = 2.55 could easily be 1.5% instead of 0.54%, which turns a 250 bps edge into a negative one. Second failure mode: the 1 cent tick. Above 0.97 the tick caps expressible edge below the 200 bps floor, so the band is narrow and the strategy may simply not fire enough to reach 60 resolved positions, in which case it is NOT_TESTED, not proven. Third: max_trades_this_window is already the single largest skip reason at 82,771 rows and 19.7% of all skips, and a strategy that wants to enter late in the window competes for exactly the slots that gate consumes. Fourth: holds of up to 5 minutes instead of 8 seconds will pressure max_concurrent_positions, which already logged 3,317 risk gate skips, which is why the 2-position cap is in the rules rather than left to the global gate.

## What past failure this addresses

Directly targets the mechanism behind hypothesis_graph ids 113 (PM_fair_value_arb, stop_too_tight), 114 (PM_fair_value_arb_hft, stop_too_tight), 136 and 137 (model_miscalibrated), 139 (PM_fair_value_arb_inverse, model_miscalibrated), 140 and 142 (stop_too_tight) and 149 (PM_fair_value_arb, spread_eats_edge), plus id 112 (PM_dip_arb, stop_too_tight) and id 141 (PM_temporal_arbitrage, entry_signal_wrong). Every one of those is a short taker round trip. This is not a rename of any of them: the entry signal is a diffusion tail probability rather than a fair value model, the exit is settlement rather than a converged mid, and the stop is a thesis-invalidation floor 13 cents below the entry band rather than an intra-window volatility stop. Vault notes leaned on: 2026-08-18-fair-value-arb-spread-problem.md (the round trip, not the model, is the cost, and do not invert a losing signal, which this does not do), fair_value_arb.md (do not lower the edge threshold to trade more, which this does not do, the gate here is stricter than the family's), and 2026-08-18-cycle-001-day-1-lessons.md (do not propose more limit-order fixes before maker fill simulation exists, which this does not, it is taker-in and settle-out). It is deliberately distinct from proposal 028 pm-status-quo-collector: 028 is blocked on a status quo classifier that does not exist, whereas this needs only strike distance and a rolling sigma, both derivable from feeds we already have.

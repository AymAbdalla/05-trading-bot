## What was built (all 4 tasks from the Raven brief)

**Task 1 — caller feed**, `strategies/polymarket/caller_feed.py` (new):
polls `redlib.catsarch.com/user/<handle>.json`, rate-limited to one poll per
handle per hour via an in-memory TTL gate (never a `sleep` — this runs inside
the same loop that drives crypto strategies on a 5-second cadence). Parses
posts into `DeclaredPlay`s with a conservative regex extractor that requires
a `$TICKER` token, a direction word, and a parseable date, ALL THREE, or
refuses the whole post with a named reason (`no_ticker_found`,
`no_direction_found`, `no_expiry_found`, `unparseable_expiry_date`,
`no_post_timestamp`). Raw posts persist to `data/caller_feed/<handle>.jsonl`;
a per-caller census persists to `data/caller_record.json`. Both files write
through `engine.concurrency` (`safe_write`/`safe_edit`), never a raw
`open(..., 'a')`.

**Task 2 — market mapper**, `strategies/polymarket/market_mapper.py` (new):
pure function `map_declared_play_checked(play, markets)` — no network call of
its own, injectable market list, so it's a genuine unit under test. Matches
on (a) ticker as a clean token in the market's slug/question, (b) `end_date`
within 1 day of the declared expiry (named `EXPIRY_TOLERANCE_DAYS`, absorbs
timezone rounding, not an economic tolerance), (c) volume above the existing
`DEFAULT_MIN_EVENT_VOLUME_USDC` floor (reused, not reinvented), (d) not
closed and active. Zero survivors AND multiple survivors both collapse to the
proposal's own `no_mapped_market_for_caller_play` — an ambiguous match is
never tiebroken by volume or anything else; the proposal is explicit that a
guess here is worse than no trade.

**Task 3 — strategy + registration**,
`strategies/polymarket/smart_money_callers.py` (new): `SmartMoneyCallers`,
`strategy_name='PM_smart_money_callers'`, fixed 5-share entries
(`CALLER_SHARES = 5`, both floor and ceiling — no Kelly scaling; see
deviation #3 below), holds to resolution (`manages_exits = False`, stop is
the structural `BINARY_STOP = 0.00`). Registered as the 21st entry
(`strategies/polymarket/__init__.py`), appended after
`MakerRebateCorridorQuoteLadder()` at index 20 — indices 0-19 untouched.
Gated on `data/caller_record.json` having ever seen the caller post
*something* parseable (`caller_record_unknown` otherwise) — this is the
literal bootstrap the proposal's "3+ declared plays" language describes,
since `verified_plays` can't move without a resolution oracle that doesn't
exist yet (see deviations).

**Task 4 — tests**: `tests/test_caller_feed.py` (443 lines),
`tests/test_market_mapper.py` (213 lines), `tests/test_smart_money_callers.py`

## Deviations from the Raven brief, and why

1. **`r.jina.ai` fallback not wired.** No verified request/response contract
   for that host this session; inventing one would be worse than not having
   a fallback. `CallerFeed._fetch_raw` is the single choke point a real
   fallback would plug into, documented with a TODO there.
2. **`supported_market_types = (MARKET_TYPE_EVENT,)`**, narrowed from the
   proposal's more general "event/sports/political" phrasing. Per
   `engine/polymarket/markets.py`'s own tag lists, `search_sports_markets`
   and `search_political_markets` walk fixed NFL/NBA/elections/Fed-style tag
   sets with nothing resembling a stock ticker — a single-stock price market
   only ever surfaces via `search_event_markets`/`search_markets`. Widening
   this later is a one-line opt-in once/if that's contradicted by something
   actually observed.
3. **Fixed 5-share sizing always**, not the proposal's Kelly-scaled bankroll
   with "first 3 plays at minimum." `MIN_SHARES == MAX_SHARES` by
   construction, so that clause is trivially satisfied — every play, first or
   hundredth, is minimum size. `caller_declared_plays_seen` /
   `caller_verified_plays` are stamped on every ENTER row so real up-sizing
   has the numbers it needs later; nothing acts on them yet. This was an
   explicit choice made in the build brief I wrote for the implementing
   session, not something the proposal asked for — flagging it here in case
   Raven wants Kelly sizing built out before this leaves index-20 obscurity.
4. **Entry gate is "caller has ANY tracked record," not "caller has 3+
   VERIFIED plays."** `verified_plays` cannot become nonzero without a
   stock-price resolution oracle, which is out of scope (see gaps below) —
   gating on a number that can structurally never move would make the
   strategy permanently and silently inert while looking like it evaluates a
   real threshold (convention 11). The proposal's actual literal 3-play
   size-up condition is unreachable until that oracle exists; this is now
   visible on-disk instead of hidden.

## Known gaps, deliberate (convention 11 — NOT_TESTED, not tested-and-failed)

- **No stock-price resolution oracle.** `CallerRecord.verified_plays` and
  `.measured` stay `0`/`False` permanently in this build. This is the same
  shape `smart_money_copy` shipped with (`wallet_record_unmeasured`) before
  `market_resolution.py` unblocked it — building the equivalent for
  individual stock tickers (not Polymarket binaries) is real, separate work.
- **One caller on the watchlist** (`CALLER_HANDLES = ('zin1422',)`, per the
  proposal's "start with one caller"). Adding a second is a watchlist edit,
  not a code change.
- **The direction-label mapping assumes Polymarket's Yes/Up side is always
  the bullish resolution.** Every observed market to date is framed that
  way; nothing here verifies the question text agrees with the label before
  trading. Documented as the single most dangerous unverified assumption in
  `smart_money_callers.py`'s module docstring — read it before touching
  `outcome_side_for_direction`.
- This strategy is D-268 NOT_TESTED like every other Polymarket strategy in
  this package until the resolution-PnL harness extension exists. Nothing
  here changes that status.
